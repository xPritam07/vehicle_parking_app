About ParkoU

Smarter Parking for a Smarter India Launched in January 2025, ParkoU is a modern solution to an age-old problem — finding reliable, safe, and flexible parking in India’s bustling cities. With urban spaces growing tighter and the need for time-based, hassle-free parking increasing every day, ParkoU steps in as your personal parking assistant. Whether you're parking for 30 minutes or several hours, ParkoU helps you choose the right plan with the right features, including: Security, Closed space parking, CCTV surveillance, Slot reservation We currently operate across multiple major Indian cities, and we're growing fast — all while focusing on convenience, transparency, and safety.

More About ParkoU

Our Vision

To make urban parking effortless, secure, and intelligently designed around your time and needs — no more circling blocks or worrying about your car while you're away.

Where We Operate

Starting from key metro cities, ParkoU is rapidly expanding its footprint across India — tailoring our parking plans to meet each city's unique needs. Whether you're in Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi, or Mumbai, there's a ParkoU plan that fits just right.

Meet the Founder

Pritam Ghosh , the founder and owner of ParkoU, is a passionate technologist and urban mobility enthusiast. With a strong background in user-centric design and systems thinking, Pritam envisioned ParkoU as a way to transform how India thinks about parking — not just as a necessity, but as a smart, accessible service.

Our Plans

From basic, affordable options to fully loaded premium plans, ParkoU offers three unique parking solutions: Park Lite, Park Smart, Park Pro.

Build for You

With an easy-to-use interface, quick booking, and clear pricing, ParkoU puts the driver in control. We're not just another parking app — we're India’s smart parking partner.
